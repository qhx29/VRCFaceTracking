# VRCFaceTracking Privacy Policy

At VRCFaceTracking, we take your privacy seriously. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

## Information Collection and Use
VRCFaceTracking does not collect or transmit telemetry, usage analytics, crash reports, or personal user data to third-party services.

Local logs may be created and stored on your device to help diagnose application issues. These logs remain local unless you choose to share them.

## Data Use and Security
VRCFaceTracking does not use or share any collected telemetry or personal data for advertising or marketing.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.
